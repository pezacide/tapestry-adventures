# Part 2: *Dream Palette* has its own repository

The design for part 2 of the series, *Dream Palette* (a Super Mario Bros. 2 style platformer set inside famous paintings), now lives in its own repository:

**https://github.com/pezacide/dream-palette** (start at `docs/DESIGN.md`)

**Series:** part 2 of **Into the Frame**, after *Ye Tapestrie Adventures* (medieval tapestries) and before *Masks & Mosaics* (antiquity). Full title: *Into the Frame: Dream Palette* (working title).

This file used to hold an early copy of that design. It had gone out of date, so it has been replaced by this note.

Part 3 of the series, *Masks & Mosaics* (antiquity), is at https://github.com/pezacide/masks-and-mosaics.
