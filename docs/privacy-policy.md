# Privacy Policy: Ye Tapestrie Adventures

*Last updated: [date]*

Ye Tapestrie Adventures ("the game") is a single-player platform game.

**What the game stores.** Your progress (which realms you have finished), your chosen settings, and whether you have bought the unlock are saved **only on your own device**. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no online features.

**What the game collects.** The game does not collect, send or sell your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, crash reports or any other personal information, and it shows **no advertising**.

**Purchases.** The optional in-app purchase ("Ye Whole Tapestrie") is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing software (a version that collects no personal data) to talk to Google Play. Unity's privacy policy is at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
