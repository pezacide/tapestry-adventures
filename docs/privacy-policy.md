# Privacy Policy: Ye Tapestrie Adventures

*Last updated: [date]*

Ye Tapestrie Adventures ("the game") is a single-player platform game by [your name or company].

**What the game stores.** Your progress (which realms you have finished), your chosen settings, and whether you have bought the unlock are saved **on your own device**. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and shows **no advertising**. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

**Purchases, and Unity.** The game offers one optional in-app purchase ("Ye Whole Tapestrie"). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details (Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy). To process the purchase, the game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing service. When the game checks or processes purchases (when you buy or restore the unlock, and when the game starts, so that it recognises a purchase you have already made), Unity's service receives:
- purchase and transaction information (the product, price and transaction details from Google Play);
- device and app identifiers;
- technical information about your device (model, operating system version, language and locale);
- your approximate location (country), which Unity works out from your internet connection's address; and
- crash and performance information from the purchasing service.

Unity uses this to run the purchase service, to avoid processing the same purchase twice, and to give us sales reports. It is encrypted in transit. Unity keeps transaction records for as long as it needs them to run the service. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

**Your choices and requests.** You can ask to see or delete the data connected to your purchases by contacting us at the address below. We will pass the request on to Unity, whose service holds it.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
